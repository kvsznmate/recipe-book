#  Creamy Coconut Curry Shrimp

### 🍤 Creamy Coconut Curry Shrimp — Step-by-Step Recipe

![Thai Coconut Curry Shrimp Recipe | HelloFresh](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/ws2P9AJmkEP57UpOD96_YKF_XxSkKZAuxqFyd-8vdunOIXJWwv-SLrdTXf5y92T5Q7VjJTSenQ-jWXCCbMN3VuO3qlIcxdy4RUdl4H4Fc7NjjFZ7IxpLY-t-CiSNKUHrrqqscy_HQhb6e9GyyYqwE47F1e-4iRotoo3a7ZWrwIXPH_JfkxTzeCYxnuM4FDAE?purpose=fullsize)

A creamy, flavourful shrimp curry made with coconut milk, garlic and spices. No red curry paste needed! This version uses everyday spices and is perfect with rice.

### 25 min

Total time

### 2

Servings

### Mild–spicy

Adjust to taste

## Ingredients

- 300 g shrimp, peeled and deveined

- 400 ml coconut milk

- 150 g rice (uncooked)

- 1 red bell pepper

- 1 small onion

- 2–3 garlic cloves

- 1 tsp paprika

- ½ tsp cumin

- ½ tsp turmeric or curry powder

- 1 tbsp soy sauce

- 1 tbsp olive oil

- ½ lime or lemon

- Chilli flakes (optional)

- Salt and black pepper

### Step-by-step instructions

![How To Cook Jasmine Rice - Everyday Delicious](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Q4eCJhpNm4ia-upiVZYasP9oxH7WR7BLfyeITmAXdFitTcIye9MtjVSqt1KcxdulW6KaLDkZQaJloj5yqGSCKFn2gnsLWp-Jhk8aSitrB4HtUY43-enfIIzFpuMqOS8UjVNTxr1NCGhik7o7Q_hd3kRw2MLOZGoT3KtHSqFhQ_fLvSsExXzFa69mwXbOfczP?purpose=fullsize)

Step 1 — Cook the rice

Rinse 150 g rice under cold water. Cook according to the packet instructions, usually 10–15 minutes for white rice. Drain if needed and set aside.

– Clean Kitchen](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/MhYScc2nYizECos4zLA8rzF9G_Z-n2gxO8l1R-D5-av6pNN5fpWBeiV1W57-JZyY-xnGtDPQsxv8vwlwL8yYu61em6M2ZcI2eid8veqP54hGyI_RDpbii5UoZrMzp-dYH4W5tfDZFAqxjM70UJNUvd4aYrTAzVcIqbx6Ok80RIvZKG1XNApF_EBf38a-qEBr?purpose=fullsize)

Step 2 — Prepare the vegetables

* Chop the onion.

* Cut the bell pepper into small strips.

* Mince the garlic.

If using fresh ginger, grate about 1 teaspoon.

![Gemüse](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Sm6rzzwmUt5MdrtwMuBIQdqvqDPxd9T16-Mzsf72_f2c4dfLKpVJkBbMc4pE0xn6u_WZb4BkZycJHrAxZbriLq3ujwf0ITiPsdnc-Ecqd3F3KiBKctTnsz-BJiTcImzITjk3fwMAPR_Yai4Adba-GUKxFfbhqr_nDGNLymLwi86iOpZWIsl1FSV0rE93sbms?purpose=fullsize)

Step 3 — Fry the vegetables

Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the onion and pepper. Fry for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.

![Shrimp Curry](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/NCcm86mSJnof8ItpmwSSpN_-6P2r0lhI_ZpxWT6WK_azkVyqB0UHkfcMxcwTlS4ajsEgY_tz3Dl7Cit_aF2kcS0-f1SwSx5K91Jzf_vSKOx-DcNxPQlsnKmEHzjJJwhJI-T6KDCmHOIYtSwZcUJFTDPFIyfbO5AO9j_XrfafVvY12O9yyh2AnIHyK5elBzOk?purpose=fullsize)

Step 4 — Add the spices

Add:

* 1 tsp paprika

* ½ tsp cumin

* ½ tsp turmeric or curry powder

* A pinch of chilli flakes, if desired

Stir for 30 seconds to release the flavours. Avoid burning the garlic or spices.

![Cobia with Thai-Style Coconut Curry Recipe | HelloFresh](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/43V_NrrdAEEQ-_iRuQnVbi400hZyNRX1WV4tdKEMvbCFc-4NpeFq9gDuMcKCOza2-AFlWiUAz3kOGdduj35FmKFJfnq6OQZ6cwO-qOAX9ytEgQpOtclDG1FzwBf4Wc9PNTgGttjAo72lXOA79ra3P7dO4SVYzFhvD1lGs3MAseZQQHfZXbUM6csLHleYrhQl?purpose=fullsize)

Step 5 — Make the coconut sauce

Pour in the 400 ml coconut milk and add 1 tablespoon soy sauce. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.

Cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly. Do not boil it aggressively.

![Thai Shrimp Curry - Food Faith Fitness](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/BLbECISaKkTHIExoftgDTaZ6EOeW9Sx6FT9JTS_sC4IcnNQ4t8YNRtbFPKZBhGjUtUXYWBjHW9tZosifqTe5LGZ7DPPOCd-2eBuB5pys2DGOrdVftkoOD5hhMELMpzWnDzPWb35Wqb2g-BtO3kW6aw8ilgUD6axPgNH55yICbSIofy-ISVkWR1pbAlOghrUo?purpose=fullsize)

Step 6 — Cook the shrimp

Add the shrimp to the sauce and stir gently.

* Raw shrimp: cook for about 3–5 minutes, depending on size.

* Already-cooked shrimp: add near the end and heat gently for 1–2 minutes.

The shrimp should be opaque throughout and cooked through. Avoid overcooking.

![Shrimp Curry: Complete Nutrition Guide - SnapCalorie](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/iQ51uY7xcv4GA1GAoJbrmG-7vWlXYPt1alOvqipJK2n08mLasC0UC_UQjgDB2Ics5WJIeYJ2wg28vxt9nBCTfsjjz3qsCjxIAkRkt7iVaVMJOXNiOquNp1SFQbDj5Bo8IEzljbbyVhr6_mP22-gBpNWKhjgiULOjeyfl6bFET6FlQ5GQcUJjaTRDKO1WHh17?purpose=fullsize)

Step 7 — Finish and serve

Turn off the heat. Squeeze in ½ lime or lemon. Taste and adjust with black pepper, salt or extra chilli.

Serve the curry over rice, spooning plenty of coconut sauce over the top.

### 👨‍🍳 Tips for the best flavour

* Too thick? Add a splash of water.

* Too thin? Simmer gently for a few more minutes before adding the shrimp.

* Want it creamier? Use full-fat coconut milk.

* Want more flavour? Add fresh ginger and finish with fresh coriander.

* Want vegetables? Add spinach in the final 1–2 minutes, or mushrooms with the peppers.

The secret: Add the lime juice at the end. Its freshness balances the creamy coconut milk and makes the whole curry taste brighter.

Enjoy your homemade coconut curry shrimp! 🥥🍤
