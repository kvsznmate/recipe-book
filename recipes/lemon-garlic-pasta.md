# Lemon Garlic Pasta

A quick weeknight pasta with lemon, garlic and parmesan. Serves 2 · 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 lemon (zest and juice)
- 40 g parmesan, grated
- A handful of parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

## Steps
1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Save a cup of the pasta water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a large pan over low heat and gently cook the garlic for 2–3 minutes without browning.
3. Add the drained pasta, lemon zest, lemon juice and a splash of pasta water. Toss well.
4. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the parmesan and parsley, and add more pasta water until glossy.
5. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
