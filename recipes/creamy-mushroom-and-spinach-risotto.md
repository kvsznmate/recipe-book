# Creamy Mushroom and Spinach Risotto

### Creamy Mushroom and Spinach Risotto

![Spinach and Mushroom Risotto Recipe | ItsOnly.Recipes](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/eynyiULrephMf9ntwknoRqEG0n3O6qo1l7xZDHVaX8bnfYBu81pmkdx4De77J_4b-jL1gkb8Gh9lrI-ItuF3J9flJuuwiNt2bSW9NMPTl1ceDe1ubBlA6NuLYw91qAZRtWR1VaJKTPpnlxbPDWz3K_Ax1lrzwjfY9536eSaqalrDZbBy-0O-NPNtKH2ODNeX?purpose=fullsize)

Let's make the original mushroom and spinach barley risotto using risotto rice (Arborio rice) instead! It will be creamier, cook faster, and still be a delicious, healthy autumn meal.

30–35 min

Total time

2 servings

Easy recipe

Vegetarian

Meat-free

### Ingredients

150–160 g Arborio risotto rice

250 g mushrooms, sliced

100 g fresh spinach

1 medium onion, finely chopped

2 garlic cloves, minced

600–750 ml vegetable stock, kept warm

1 tbsp olive oil

20–30 g grated Parmesan cheese

½ tsp dried thyme or 1 tsp fresh thyme

Black pepper to taste

A squeeze of lemon juice (optional)

### Step-by-step instructions

![크리미 머쉬룸 파스타 | 부드러운 크림과 버섯 풍미](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/z1jfEIUparGiAS9cHRbfWJvy1EBqgpdh5dBFLUPsVlSIJpeJACl7bbF_q6c2yieMzyNx1NRFJKsJj21yGsWR7EJyiX2udWYJI9eBgkB4ZoQ_Rky6GyE4DzKN_jH5J3_Mj9okVnWjjMhBz_Jujh0x_OrcgrxW5hWJzT1AX2hsLN5hclc5Q2E6h0fn-92wGrH3?purpose=fullsize)

Step 1 — Cook the mushrooms

5–7 minutes

Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 2–3 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden. Stir in the garlic and thyme for another 30–60 seconds.

![Risotto de setas, clásica y cremosa receta italiana fácil de hacer](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/unUBb1XpHKx4YvT4vP6SBkzANfpu7yNHgfS0StMmyNQ70ISR8joxx5FPqQIZwVHPcZP6xolShxLNqAZMIW6ZA6b8JSiwXLk0kPQ9ObrRGOJo2S5H7bZSF0Nh-ydiAF0EEmcBkRdEqtL3ZXMY67WjJatetzTtXkbHqd_6qGYDq-f1zeboBSxAeSj0tjRuSnIH?purpose=fullsize)

Step 2 — Toast the risotto rice

1–2 minutes

Add the uncooked risotto rice directly to the pan. Stir well so the grains are coated in the oil and mushroom juices. Don't rinse the rice; its surface starch helps make the risotto creamy.

![Risotto crémeux aux champignons (420 kcal, 40 min)](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/TVZ1lTnO5LhCwAcGvA7e7v9FzsGxzbGhJc8Ph5ugV0KXhclP3mNhCAHfH7_r60ohFEa2BMuKGO4ZPcL1k-Bj5H11-ntaoKCvrIHLzqrKcXKBjL_kRLgxZo-22eN4LYRWhCEDVhQi1bLbXtAkfN2OLML1CT9k5vuNhpEHRaWQ_i2Si0mRvLvBS89Astipmexu?purpose=fullsize)

Step 3 — Add the stock gradually

18–25 minutes

Add one ladle of warm vegetable stock and stir. When most of the liquid has been absorbed, add another ladle. Continue gradually until the rice is creamy and tender with a slight bite. You may not need all the stock.

![Cremiges Risotto mit Champignons Rezept | HelloFresh](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/lb19_Mhufw03UD-awUVRhNPh_GddeJMQkiurS09IBlqnjR40cw207rIdoCAqA2QEW4ksdi3ux0qli_y-3CwUyOkrHg-wEzY_Zf2O9JyDiVoVZzFARITneCbrOp_Ey__gkcBTfjnTidxv5jXZ87XX-PzvtKy57RUYZqXKyrHWv7kgXbGnt7GyzDdjHElmFJ_-?purpose=fullsize)

Step 4 — Add the spinach

2 minutes

Stir in the fresh spinach until wilted. If the risotto looks too thick, add a little more warm stock.

![Cremiger Spinat und Pilzrisotto Schnell und Einfach - Recipe Website](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/pa9KpZqr6EMSgOWHvL4IJQ8NlEPlDqmOxH5Ql5EO1V0H7Y_PaudB4v6VBNoxfZ1oAnBzcBEMKIEwcN0W4VnG0R-SFRtw5DMJdhY3wzjo1xVIgS3AnXw6m1CbIwuKURuMInGGn-ElI8Rl8TtYAXhcU4TDxBNQqZnGEHbYFwIOw6wByUnSnww4h_bQLBdVgNzu?purpose=fullsize)

Step 5 — Make it creamy

2 minutes

Turn off the heat. Stir in the Parmesan and season with black pepper. Add a little lemon juice if you like. Let it rest for 1–2 minutes, then serve immediately.
