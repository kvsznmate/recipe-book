# Hungarian Pancakes/Crepes (Palacsinta)

Hungarian Pancakes (Palacsinta)

![Thin, traditional crêpes](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Crepes%20dsc07085.jpg)

Thin, soft, and delicious, Hungarian pancakes (palacsinta) are similar to French crêpes. They can be served with jam, chocolate spread, cinnamon sugar, or sweetened cottage cheese.

Recipe Information

- Preparation time: 5 minutes
- Cooking time: 40 minutes
- Resting time: 30 minutes
- Total time: Approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes
- Yield: Approximately 20 pancakes

Ingredients

- 400 g all-purpose flour
- 4 eggs
- 600 ml milk
- 400 ml sparkling water
- 2 pinches of salt
- Vegetable oil, for frying

Instructions

1. Prepare the batter

Combine the flour, salt, eggs, and half of the milk in a large mixing bowl. Whisk until smooth and free of lumps.

2. Add the remaining milk

Gradually pour in the remaining milk while whisking continuously.

3. Add the sparkling water

Pour in the sparkling water and mix until the batter is smooth and thin, similar to heavy cream.

4. Let the batter rest

Cover the bowl and let the batter rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.

5. Adjust the consistency

Stir the batter again before cooking. If it is too thick, add a little more sparkling water.

6. Heat the pan

Lightly grease a non-stick frying pan with vegetable oil and heat it over medium heat.

7. Cook the crêpes

Pour a small ladleful of batter into the pan. Immediately tilt and rotate the pan to spread the batter into a thin, even layer.

Cook until the edges begin to lift and the underside turns golden. Flip the crêpe and cook the other side until lightly browned.

Repeat with the remaining batter, lightly greasing the pan as needed.

Serving Suggestions

Serve warm with your favourite filling:

- Apricot or strawberry jam
- Chocolate or hazelnut spread
- Cinnamon sugar
- Sweetened cottage cheese
- Ground walnuts and powdered sugar

Roll each crêpe around its filling or fold it into quarters.

Tips for Perfect Crêpes

- Whisk the batter thoroughly to prevent lumps.
- Let the batter rest for at least 30 minutes.
- Swirl the batter around the pan immediately after pouring.
- Use medium heat to prevent excessive browning.
- Stack the cooked crêpes on a plate and cover them to keep them soft.

Enjoy your homemade Hungarian palacsinta! 🥞
