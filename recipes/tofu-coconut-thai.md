# Tofu coconut thai

Thai-Inspired Coconut Tofu Curry

Prep time: 10 minutes
Cooking time: 25 minutes
Servings: 2

🛒 Ingredients

* 400 g firm tofu
* 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
* 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
* 1 red bell pepper
* 1 small broccoli
* 1 small onion
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp fresh grated ginger (optional)
* Juice of ½ lime
* 150 g uncooked jasmine rice
* Fresh coriander (optional)

👩🏻‍🍳 Instructions

1. Cook the rice

* Rinse the rice under cold water.
* Cook according to the package instructions.

2. Prepare the tofu

* Drain the tofu and pat it dry with paper towels.
* Cut into bite-sized cubes.

3. Fry the tofu

* Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat.
* Add the tofu and fry for 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden.
* Remove the tofu from the pan and set aside.

4. Cook the vegetables

* Chop the onion, bell pepper, and broccoli into bite-sized pieces.
* In the same pan, sauté the onion for 2–3 minutes.
* Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another 30 seconds.
* Add the bell pepper and broccoli and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.

5. Make the curry sauce

* Add the red curry paste and stir for 1 minute.
* Pour in the coconut milk and mix well.
* Add the soy sauce and bring to a gentle simmer.
* Cook for 8–10 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.

6. Add the tofu

* Return the tofu to the pan.
* Simmer for another 2–3 minutes so the tofu absorbs the flavours.
* Add the lime juice and stir.

7. Serve

* Spoon the curry over jasmine rice.
* Garnish with fresh coriander and an extra squeeze of lime.
